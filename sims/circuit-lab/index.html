Quick reference

  • Ohm's law V = IR, I = V/R, R = V/I
  • Series R Rtotal = R₁ + R₂ + …
  • Parallel R 1/Req = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …
  • Series I same current through all components
  • Parallel V same voltage across all branches
  • Power P = IV = I²R = V²/R

About Circuit Lab

  1. Placing components. Click a component button in the toolbar (Battery, Resistor, Bulb, Switch), then click on the grid to place it. Press R to rotate before placing.
  2. Drawing wires. Select the Wire tool (or press W), click a component terminal (the dot at each end), then click another terminal to connect them with a wire.
  3. Adjusting values. In Select mode, click a component to select it. A slider appears below the canvas to change its resistance or voltage.
  4. Switches. Click any switch to toggle it on/off. An open switch breaks the circuit and stops current flow.
  5. Circuit analysis. When you build a complete circuit (battery + components + wires forming a closed loop), the side panel shows total resistance, current, power, and per-component readings.
  6. Practice mode. Switch to Practice to solve randomly generated series and parallel circuit problems. Calculate total resistance, current, or voltage drops.
Tip: Start simple — place a battery and a resistor, then wire their terminals in a loop. Add a bulb to see it glow when current flows!
